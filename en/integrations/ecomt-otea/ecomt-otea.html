ECOMT OTEA platform

EcoMT handles the digital management of facilities from start to finish. It designs and installs a custom control panel at each one that connects HVAC, lighting, industrial refrigeration, photovoltaics and energy, water or gas meters, and integrates any equipment, whatever its brand or age.

The panels connect to OTEA Software, its energy management and remote operation platform, to monitor in real time, control remotely and automate with rules, with lower consumption, early detection of faults and better comfort. A Control Centre supervises day-to-day operation, produces reports, prioritises incidents and applies AI analysis.

Integration

MICA sends its measurements over LoRaWAN to a gateway. The PLC in EcoMT's control panel reads that data from the gateway via Modbus TCP and, through OTEA Edge, sends it to OTEA Software, which stores it and keeps its history.

Once in OTEA, the air quality data has several uses.

  • View it in real time and historically, by facility or by groups of facilities.
  • Use it to trigger alerts by WhatsApp, SMS or email when a variable exceeds a threshold.
  • Combine it with rules that act on ventilation or air conditioning.
  • Send it to the customer's other tools (ERP, CMMS, Power BI) through the OTEA API.
Company

EcoMT

EcoMT is a control engineering firm specialised in the digital management of facilities. Since 2010 it has made its customers' facilities provide the data needed, warn when something fails and consume only what is needed, all with a single supplier. It manages more than 10,000 facilities in 86 countries for companies such as Inditex, Correos, Aldi, Lupa and Repsol.
