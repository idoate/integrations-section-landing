Sauter Systems

Sauter Systems specializes in building automation and climate control management. MICA's indoor air measurement optimizes HVAC performance and ensures comfortable, efficient environments.

How to integrate it
Connectivity
Modbus
Integration

Sauter's controllers register the MICA as just another Modbus input on the BMS, and from there they can make climate control conditional whenever CO2 or the ventilation index falls out of range, the same way they already do with the installation's temperature or humidity. That same data stays visible on the building's management panel, so the maintenance team can compare energy consumption against real air quality before adjusting ventilation schedules or scheduling a checkup.

Company

Sauter Ibérica (Fr. Sauter AG)

Sauter Ibérica is the Spanish subsidiary of Fr. Sauter AG, a Swiss group headquartered in Basel and founded in 1910, specializing in building automation (BMS), systems integration, and facility management. The group has more than 4,000 employees and a presence in more than 20 countries.
