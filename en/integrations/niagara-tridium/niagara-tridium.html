Niagara Tridium

Niagara (Tridium) is a framework designed to integrate a building's IoT elements to generate automations, schedules, and alarms, and support decision-making. MICA data is integrated into Niagara's BMS and can be visualized from there in its platform.

How to integrate it
Connectivity
Modbus
Integration

Over Modbus, the Niagara system (JACE) periodically reads the MICA's air quality registers and incorporates them into the framework alongside the rest of the building's systems. With that data, the installer can visualize air quality in real time and configure automations, schedules, and alarms based on its thresholds.

Company

Niagara Framework (Tridium)

Niagara Framework is a software platform developed by Tridium (a company acquired by Honeywell International in 2005) for developing and integrating connected devices and systems in buildings. It allows BMS integrators to connect equipment from different manufacturers using open and proprietary protocols (BACnet, Modbus, LonWorks, among others) from a single platform; it has more than a million installations worldwide.
