Integration

Kiona receives the MICA's data through an API webhook, though its platform is built to adapt to other communication protocols when the installation calls for it. Its philosophy is to integrate any building system regardless of brand, vendor, or age, and air quality data fits that same logic: it's added to the rest of the systems to feed the intelligent analysis Kiona uses to detect anomalies and adjustment opportunities before they become a problem.

Company

Kiona

Kiona is a Nordic provider (headquartered in Trondheim, Norway) of SaaS solutions for building digitalization and energy consumption optimization, part of the CAREL group since July 2023. Its platform combines AI and big data for managing commercial, industrial, and multi-residential buildings.
