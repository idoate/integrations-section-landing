Miniserver Loxone

Loxone is a comprehensive automation system for homes and buildings based on the Miniserver. MICA's air quality data automatically triggers ventilation and climate control to maintain healthy spaces.

How to integrate it
Connectivity
Modbus
Integration

Loxone incorporates the MICA as just another Modbus device within the Miniserver, making it possible to build simple, highly varied automations from its data: triggering mechanical ventilation when CO2 rises or the ventilation index drops, or interacting with windows, blinds, or other elements of the installation based on the rest of the indicators. That's Loxone's hallmark: extending and automating any installation with maximum flexibility, which here translates into cross-referencing air quality with the energy-saving and security programs the Miniserver already manages.

Company

Loxone

The Miniserver is the central controller of Loxone's home and building automation systems, connecting and coordinating all devices (lighting, climate control, blinds, audio, etc.) for comprehensive control and automation. It runs locally without depending on the cloud, with several hundred thousand projects deployed worldwide.
