Cirkuit

Cirkuit provides support for long range smart building networks to enable real-time alerting and data collection for building optimization and risk reduction. Cirkuit helps our clients procure, deploy and operate these building networks end-to-end, with elegant wireless solutions enabling rapid installation, sometimes having buildings online and collecting data within hours. Our web platform supports formal IAQ reporting (LEED, BOMA Best, etc) and enables details controls for sensor alerting for environmental conditions or building activity.

Integration

The Cirkuit platform receives sensor data from the MICA device at regular intervals, collected entirely wirelessly at a frequency set by the customer. From here, the data can be viewed via dashboards or interrogated using an interactive AI chat conversation. Sensor data is also available via their API, and can be used to trigger alerts from the system when specific environmental conditions are breached (e.g. high temperature detected).

Company

Cirkuit

Cirkuit was founded in 2020 in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. The mission of the company is to make smart buildings simple, fast and cost-effective. Their current primary market is the commercial real estate space, including office, retail, warehouse and multi-family housing. They work with everyone from local property management companies to global real estate giants managing large portfolios of diverse property types.
