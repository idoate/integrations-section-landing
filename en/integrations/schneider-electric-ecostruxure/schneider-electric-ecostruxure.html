Schneider Electric EcoStruxure

Schneider Electric EcoStruxure is Schneider Electric's IoT architecture for energy and building management. Integrating MICA's air quality data helps balance energy efficiency with occupant health, while also providing access to the KNX ecosystem.

How to integrate it
Connectivity
Modbus
Integration

Schneider Electric's equipment reads the MICA's data over Modbus to regulate the building's HVAC systems, mainly ventilation, adjusting it according to the indoor air quality's ventilation indicator. Once integrated into Schneider's system, that same data also becomes available to the rest of the KNX ecosystem's devices and to IoT device platforms such as SmartThings Pro.

Company

Schneider Electric

EcoStruxure Building is Schneider Electric's open IoT platform for buildings, which securely connects hardware, software, and services over an IP network to maximize efficiency and comfort. It includes solutions such as EcoStruxure Building Operation (BMS for large buildings) and EcoStruxure Building Activate (energy management for smaller buildings), geared toward monitoring, control, and energy optimization of facilities.
