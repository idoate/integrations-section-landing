Akenza.io

Akenza is a self-service IoT platform for connecting, managing, and analyzing devices. It integrates MICA's air quality data to create custom rules, alerts, and applications.

How to integrate it
Connectivity
LoRa
Integration

The MICA sends its data over LoRa to a gateway that routes it to the Akenza platform, though inBiot and Akenza already have experience working together to adapt the integration to other protocols when the project requires it. Once there, the air quality values are integrated into Akenza's 3D maps (designed to show at a glance how each space is doing) and feed its rule engine, which is used to build workflows and automatic triggers from the MICA's measurements.

Company

Akenza

Akenza is a Swiss IoT platform (headquartered in Zurich, founded in 2017), low-code/self-service in nature, that connects, manages, and visualizes sensor data across buildings and industrial operations, supporting protocols such as LoRaWAN, MQTT, HTTP, and CoAP. It holds ISO 27001 certification and counts clients such as ISS Switzerland and Zurich Insurance.
